# Regional Manager quick guide

**Your job each month:** revise your region's forecast, then agree your allocation and finalize it.

### Part 1: review (cycle state REGIONAL_REVIEW)
1. Open your dashboard. Only your region's column can be edited.
2. Use **Sales analysis**: 3, 6, 12 and 24-month averages, the same month last year, and the trend chart. Click a product to see its monthly history.
3. Revise quantities. Changes bigger than the change threshold need a reason.
4. **Submit** your region before the review deadline shown in the cycle bar.

### Part 2: allocation (cycle state REGIONAL_FINALIZATION)
5. Open your allocation. **My fill rate** shows how much of your demand is covered.
6. Need more of a product? **Request** it from another region. Have spare? **Offer** it. The other region must
   **Accept** or **Reject**, with a reason. Moves apply only when accepted, and totals never change.
7. **Export xlsx** or **Export csv** if you need the numbers elsewhere.
8. **Finalize** your region before the finalize deadline. You cannot finalize while a transfer you are part of is
   pending. **Un-finalize** reopens your region until the whole cycle is FINAL.

Rules that surprise people:
- You can only see and change your own region's numbers and sales.
- When every region finalizes, the cycle locks (FINAL). After that, only an Admin can reopen it.
